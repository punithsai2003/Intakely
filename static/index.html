Pre-visit intake for clinics

Smart intake before every visit

Intakely is a pre-visit intake assistant. Before the appointment, the patient chooses their doctor and answers a few simple questions in English, Hindi or Telugu, one at a time. Intakely turns those answers into a short, organised summary for the doctor: the main complaint, how long it has lasted, medicines, allergies and history.

Safety comes first. Emergency warning signs are caught by fixed rules, and the patient sees a clear message to call 108 straight away. AI only helps read and organise what the patient wrote, and every detail must come from the patient's own words. An accuracy check blocks any summary that doesn't match their answers. The doctor stays in control and marks each summary as reviewed.

About us

Less paperwork, more time with the patient

Why Intakely exists

Every consultation starts the same way: the doctor asks the same basic questions, and the patient tries to remember everything under pressure. That time could go to examination, medication decisions and listening. Intakely moves the routine questions before the visit so the conversation can focus on the patient.

Who built it

What we believe

  • Doctors decide.AI organises information. It never diagnoses or advises.
  • Safety is rule-based.Emergency detection does not depend on AI guesswork.
  • Everything is traceable.Each detail in a summary comes from something the patient actually said.
  • Language is not a barrier.Patients answer in the language they are most comfortable with.

For patients

Tell your doctor before you walk in

It takes about three minutes. Choose your doctor, then answer eight short questions, one on each screen. Your answers go only to the doctor you pick.

If anything you describe sounds like an emergency, you will be told to call 108 straight away.
